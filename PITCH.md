# LEARNOVA AI — Hackathon Pitch Script (3 Minutes)

**Total Time:** 180 Seconds | **Speakers:** 3

---

## 🎤 Speaker 1: The Visionary (0:00 - 1:00)

**Focus:** _Innovation & Real-World Impact_

"Good morning, judges. We are the team behind **LEARNOVA AI**.

Most students today use AI as a shortcut—a 'copy-paste' tool that gives answers but kills curiosity. We built Learnova to do the opposite. It’s an adaptive, multimodal tutor that doesn't just give answers; it uses **Socratic questioning** to help you _understand_.

What makes us truly innovative? Our native support for **Banglish**. Millions of students think and communicate in a mix of English and Bangla. While other platforms struggle, Learnova understands this cultural nuance, breaking the language barrier in education.

Our impact is measurable: we aren't just building a chatbot; we're building a bridge for the 40 million students in Bangladesh and beyond who need personalized, affordable, and accessible education. Learnova turns every smartphone into a world-class private tutor."

---

## 🎤 Speaker 2: The Architect (1:00 - 2:00)

**Focus:** _Technical Execution & Scalability_

"Building a system this responsive required a **production-grade architecture**.

We utilized **React 19 and TanStack Start** for a seamless full-stack experience. At the core of our intelligence is a **Multi-LLM Failover Gateway**. We route high-speed requests through **Groq** and fallback to **Gemini 2.5 Flash** for complex reasoning. This ensures 99.9% uptime and sub-second response times.

Our system is truly **multimodal**.

- With **'Paper to Podcast'**, we transform static PDFs into engaging audio discussions.
- With **'Draw to Learn'**, our vision-integrated AI analyzes hand-drawn diagrams in real-time.
- And our **'Brain Battles'** utilize Supabase Realtime for low-latency, 1v1 competitive learning.

This isn't a prototype; it’s a robust, modular system designed to scale from one student to a million, hosted on a global edge infrastructure."

---

## 🎤 Speaker 3: The Strategist (2:00 - 3:00)

**Focus:** _Business Model, Global Readiness & Ethics_

"Innovation needs a sustainable engine. Our **Business Model** follows a 'Freemium' pathway: providing essential tutoring for free while offering advanced multimodal features and deeper analytics via a subscription model.

We are **Globally Ready**. Our architecture supports international curriculum standards, and we’ve designed Learnova for **NRB (Non-Resident Bangladeshi) collaboration**, allowing global mentors to contribute to our knowledge base.

**Ethics** is at our core. We’ve implemented strict system prompts to prevent AI hallucinations and ensure data privacy. We don't just filter bias; we actively train our tutor to be inclusive.

In conclusion, Learnova AI is more than a project—it’s a scalable, economically viable, and ethically sound solution to the global education crisis.

**Learnova AI: Where Curiosity Meets Intelligence.** Thank you."
